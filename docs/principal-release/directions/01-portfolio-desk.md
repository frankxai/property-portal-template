# Direction 01 — Portfolio desk (selected)

Grounding: advanced portal commit b644384, its owner/control-center specification, and fresh desktop/mobile captures of the Vercel baseline. The existing first screen spends most of its area on a sample photograph and a promise; it does not expose a financial decision.

Use a compact left navigation, a portfolio table, a cash-flow trajectory, and a prioritized decision column. Open an asset into an editable underwriting sheet. Financial consequences stay adjacent to inputs. Restrained teal marks positive operating cash, amber marks assumptions requiring attention. Data uses tabular system numerals. All example records are identified as fictional.

This direction serves a technically experienced owner who wants to inspect and change a model immediately. Primary action: stress the portfolio. No decorative motion.
