# Direction 03 — Investment notebook

Grounding: existing property workspace/runbooks and the baseline's property-first presentation, inspected at desktop and mobile.

An individual acquisition memo becomes the main canvas: thesis, operating model, downside, evidence, and owner decision. Structured annotations sit beside a long-form argument, with a scenario comparison at the end. Strong for an investment committee, but serial and slower for a multi-asset operating owner. Rejected as the primary navigation; its exportable decision brief is retained as a supporting artifact.
